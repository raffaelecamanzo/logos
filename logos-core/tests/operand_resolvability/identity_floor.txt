THE FLOOR
---------

    >= 16 NET-NEW identity-resolved consumer-to-provider pairs
       on the pec-services estate.

"Net-new" means, exactly: an identity-resolved consumer->provider pair that
path-only matching (normalized positional template + HTTP method + the
exactly-one-candidate rule of FR-CG-09) would NOT already have bound -- because
path-only ties between two or more providers, or matches no provider at all.

A pair that path-only matching would already have bound alone is NOT counted
toward this floor. It is reported, but it is not evidence for identity.

Measured net-new < 16  =>  FALSIFIED.
Measured net-new >= 16 =>  the gate holds and the dependent stories may be planned.


WHY 16 — DERIVED FROM CR-121'S OWN FIGURES
------------------------------------------

1. CR-121 section 6 states the product under test verbatim: "On the reference
   workspace, resolved identity-based cross-service REST edges rise from 0 to at
   least 31", with roughly 15 path-only candidates reported separately. 31 is the
   hand-computed number this gate exists to test. CR-121 already separates the ~15
   path-only candidates from the 31, so the 31 is intended to be identity's OWN
   product -- the claim being tested is therefore net-new ~= 31.

2. The floor is one half of that claim: 31 / 2 = 15.5, rounded up to 16.
   Below half, the hand computation is wrong by more than a factor of two. That is
   precisely the failure mode that killed CR-113 (CR-121 section 7 names it: "This
   is exactly how CR-113 died"), and CR-121 section 7's own mitigation for this
   risk is to stop after the configuration milestone and ship resolution at
   candidate confidence only.

3. 16 clears the single-member-coincidence threshold. FR-WS-20's Notes record that
   ONE aggregator's deploy manifest names SEVEN callees explicitly. A result at or
   below ~7 would be that one manifest and nothing else -- an estate coincidence,
   not an estate-wide capability, and not a basis for eight stories that build an
   entire identity + egress + tiering + surfaces milestone whose only product is
   these edges. 16 requires the deploy-manifest -> via-key -> call-site join to
   work well beyond that single aggregator.

4. Symmetry with the band CR-121 sets for itself. CR-121 section 7 declares an
   UPPER kill signal for the overlay-union decision at "more than three times the
   hand-computed edge count". It declares no lower band. The symmetric lower band
   would be 31/3 = 10.3; 16 is deliberately stricter than that, because the upper
   band guards against noise whereas the lower band guards against the capability
   not existing at all, and the latter costs eight stories.

5. The floor is a count of PAIRS, not of members, labels or keys, because the pair
   is what CR-121 promises and what the eight gated stories deliver. Reporting a
   high self-identity yield (AC1) or a high via-key resolution rate (AC2) while
   producing few net-new pairs does NOT clear this floor. Only AC3's net-new
   figure is measured against it.


WHAT IS *NOT* A FLOOR
---------------------

AC1 (self identity per member and tier, same-tier collisions), AC2 (via-key
resolution: to a call site / to a member / to an external label / to nothing, with
unmatched labels enumerated) and AC4 (pairs differing per overlay) are REPORTS.
They are required for the finding to be adjudicable and they attribute a
falsification to whichever join broke, but none of them can clear or fail this
gate on its own. There is exactly one decisive number, so it cannot be cleared by
substituting a more favourable statistic.


COMMITMENT
----------

This floor is not revised after the run. If the measurement shows the floor was
badly chosen, that is stated in the implementation notes with the reasoning, and
THIS FILE STANDS UNCHANGED.
