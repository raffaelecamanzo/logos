THE FLOOR
---------

    >= 7 of the 13 PRODUCTION (src/main) broker publish sites resolve their topic
       operand with forwarding bounded at TWO CALL FRAMES, WITHIN THE OPERAND'S OWN
       BUILD MODULE, with call-site agreement taken over src/main CALL SITES ONLY.

That is one reading and one number. The 2x2 grid below is printed in full, but only
this cell carries the floor:

                         agreement over EVERY      agreement over src/main
                         in-module call site       call sites only
    one frame            reported (S-392: 0)       reported (S-392: 6)
    two frames           reported                  ** THE FLOOR: >= 7 of 13 **

"Resolve at two frames" means, exactly and conjunctively:

  (a) the site's topic operand is a BARE PARAMETER of the enclosing method — the same
      classifier S-392 used (`resolve_key` returning `Refusal::MethodParameter`), not a
      literal, not a field, not a composed expression;
  (b) that method has at least one call site in the SAME BUILD MODULE, in src/main;
  (c) at each such call site the matching positional argument is EITHER terminal — a
      literal, or a `${…}`/`@ConfigurationProperties` accessor that FR-WS-19 resolves
      against committed configuration — OR itself a bare parameter of the method
      enclosing that call site, in which case ONE further frame is taken: the callers
      of THAT method, again in the same build module and in src/main, again read
      positionally;
  (d) every value so obtained AGREES. Disagreement at either frame emits per-site
      candidates and NO edge, so a disagreeing site does NOT count toward this floor;
  (e) NO THIRD FRAME. An argument that is still a bare parameter after two frames is a
      refusal, named as such, and does NOT count.

Measured two-frame main-only resolved < 7  =>  FALSIFIED.
Measured two-frame main-only resolved >= 7 =>  the gate holds and S-417 may be planned.


WHY 7 — THE SAME NUMBER, ON THE SAME METRIC, AS S-392
-----------------------------------------------------

The floor is NOT re-derived here, and that is deliberate. CR-131 §3.2 C1 states it as
"at least 7 of the 13 production publish sites resolve, **on S-392's own metric**",
and S-392's `forwarding_floor.txt` derived 7 three ways over the same population:

  1. One half of CR-121 §6's own claim — "the 13 production publish sites resolve" —
     rounded up: 13 / 2 = 6.5 -> 7. The same rule S-384's identity floor applied.
  2. A strict majority of 13. Below half, the mechanism is the EXCEPTION on this
     estate and the requirement would be writing a minority idiom into a Should.
  3. A count of PRODUCTION SITES RESOLVED — not wrappers, not call sites, not members,
     not agreeing operands — because the site is what becomes a `Producer` node.

Re-deriving a floor for a re-proposal is how a gate gets quietly lowered to fit what
the second attempt can reach. S-393's closure fixed the re-proposal bar as "the two-hop
case in scope with its own justification and its own measurement, or a corrected
call-site rule that excludes test-tree stubs — each measured, not assumed". Both are in
scope here and both are measured; the BAR does not move because the mechanism did.

What has changed, and is stated openly rather than left for a reader to notice: the
decisive reading is now the FAVOURABLE one. S-392 reported 0 under the criterion as
written and 6 under the main-only reading, and its falsification held under BOTH — which
is why it was robust. This gate rests on a single cell, chosen in advance by CR-131 §3.2
C1 on the stated ground that a Mockito stub is not a publish. A pass here therefore
licenses LESS than S-392's failure refuted: it says the two-frame main-only rule clears
the bar, and says nothing about the rule as S-392 wrote it. The other three cells are
printed beside it so that a reader can see exactly how much of the result the two
relaxations bought, one at a time.


WHAT IS *NOT* A FLOOR — THE REPORTS
-----------------------------------

Required for the finding to be adjudicable, and for a falsification to be attributable
to whichever join broke. NONE of them can clear or fail this gate:

  * THE OTHER THREE CELLS of the grid. One frame / every call site is S-392's headline
    and is expected to reproduce at 0. One frame / main-only is expected at 6. Two
    frames / every call site is the strict reading of the new bound. A strong figure in
    any of them does not clear this floor, and a weak one does not fail it.

  * THE RECONCILIATION OF S-392's SEVEN NAMED TWO-FRAME SITES. S-392 named them, with
    the src/main call site that decides each:

        archive-listener-adapter         ArchiveEventKafkaProducer.java:16
        deprecated-mailbox-core/manager  DelayedMessageKafkaProducer.java:23
        pecserver-facade                 PecServerOperationKafkaProducer.java:23
        archive-manager                  DelayedMessageKafkaProducer.java:21
        mailbox-manager                  DelayedMessageKafkaProducer.java:23
        official-log-export-job-worker   OfficialLogOutcomeKafkaProducer.java:17
        official-log-export-job-manager  DelayedMessageKafkaProducer.java:19

    Each is reconciled BY NAME against this run: resolved at two frames, or refused with
    its reason. A site S-392 named that this run does not even see is a harness drift and
    makes the run VOID, not falsified (V5 below). This reconciliation is EVIDENCE for the
    headline, not a second headline: the gate is decided by the count of 13, not by how
    many of the 7 land.

  * THE REFUSAL CENSUS. Every refusal is enumerated with its reason — three or more
    frames, out-of-module caller, disagreeing callers, no call site, an argument that
    resolves to nothing. It is the explanation of the headline, not a second headline.

  * THE NAMED MOCKITO EXCLUSIONS. The main-tree rule excludes test-tree call sites from
    the agreement. Every call site it excludes is PRINTED with its file, line and
    resolved value — named, never silently dropped. This is what makes "a Mockito stub is
    not a publish" an auditable claim rather than an assertion: a reader can see exactly
    which sites the relaxation removed and judge for themselves whether each is a stub.
    A run that reports a pass without printing them has not reported the pass.

  * THE PARSE-AND-RESOLVE COST, over its denominator, separating timed work from
    measurement scaffolding. A slow hop is a budget conversation, not a falsification.

  * THE TEST-TREE ARM and the CLIENT-CALL ARM. Reported beside the production figure and
    never summed with it, for the reason Sprint 65 established: averaging the two trees
    is how CR-117 looked validated at arm level while being false on the population its
    criterion was written over.


NON-VACUITY PRECONDITIONS — "VOID" IS NOT "FALSIFIED"
-----------------------------------------------------

A zero produced by a walk that walked nothing reads identically to a zero produced by a
walk that walked everything. The run is VOID — neither confirming nor falsifying, and
the gate must be re-run — unless ALL of these hold, each asserted by the harness:

  V1. The walk admits at least 2 estate members, and a non-zero number of Java files
      and publish-carrying files (S-392's V1/V2).
  V2. The walk finds AT LEAST 13 production (src/main) publish sites, and the production
      broker candidate population is EXACTLY 13 — the denominator this floor is stated
      over (S-392's V3, plus its candidate-count assertion).
  V3. Every publish site the authority recogniser found is joined to an AST node, and
      every bare-parameter operand is given a positional slot (S-392's
      `sites_without_a_node` and `operands_without_a_slot`, both zero).
  V4. The `Calls` ledger yields a non-zero number of call rows across the estate.
  V5. THE ONE-FRAME READINGS REPRODUCE S-392 EXACTLY: 0 under the criterion as written
      and 6 under the main-only reading, with the two-or-more-hop residue at 7 and the
      test-stub-blocked count at 6. If the one-frame figures have moved, the two-frame
      figure is not measuring the increment this gate is about, and the run is VOID.
      This is the single strongest non-vacuity condition available here and it is
      deliberately an EQUALITY: the second frame is an increment over a fixed base, and
      an increment over a base that moved is not the quantity CR-131 CRA-03 asserts.

  V6. THE SECOND FRAME DOES SOMETHING. At least one candidate resolves at two frames
      that did not resolve at one, under the same call-site rule. A two-frame reading
      that equals its one-frame reading has not measured a second frame; it has measured
      a no-op, and a floor cleared by a no-op is cleared by the FIRST frame's 6 plus an
      arithmetic accident. VOID, not a pass.


A CAVEAT DECLARED IN ADVANCE, NOT A FLOOR
-----------------------------------------

S-392 declared it and it applies here with more force, because the second frame reaches
further into one idiom: if the resolved sites funnel through a SINGLE wrapper method, or
sit in a SINGLE member, the inference generalises correspondingly less. The harness
reports the number of DISTINCT wrapper methods, DISTINCT forwarding chains and DISTINCT
members behind the resolved count, whatever the verdict. Stated here, before the run, so
it is read as a declared limit on what a PASS licenses — not as a second threshold
invented afterwards to argue away an inconvenient result. It does not clear or fail the
gate.

A second caveat, specific to two frames: the bound is POSITIONAL, INTRA-MODULE,
MAIN-TREE and REFUSES ON DISAGREEMENT. It is not dataflow, it has no fixpoint, and a
pass here licenses exactly two frames and not three. CR-131 §7 records "the two-frame
bound is read as a licence for general dataflow" as a risk against ADR-64; the figure
this run produces must not be cited for any bound other than the one measured.
