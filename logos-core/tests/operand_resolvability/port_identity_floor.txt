THE FLOOR
---------

    >= 16 NET-NEW THIRD-PARTY port-resolved consumer-to-provider call-site edges
       on the pec-services estate.

Three words in that sentence carry the whole definition, and each is fixed here
rather than at measurement time.

"CALL-SITE EDGE" means, exactly: one distinct

    (consumer member, normalized route template, provider member)

triple — the identical grain S-384's `Findings::net_new` counts, so that the two
experiments are directly comparable. CR-124's Decision Log fixes this unit
explicitly and rejects the alternative: "16 net-new SERVICE PAIRS (census suggests
9 — likely falsification) ... a pair-based floor would reject on a unit S-384 never
measured." Two call sites in one class reading one key at one template are ONE
edge. The distinct member-to-member coupling count is a different, smaller figure;
it is reported beside the headline and it does NOT clear or fail this gate.

"NET-NEW" means, exactly: an edge that path-only matching — normalized positional
template plus the exactly-one-candidate rule of FR-CG-09 — would NOT already have
bound, because two or more members register that template and the exactly-one rule
therefore refuses. An edge the path alone would already have bound is reported in
its own column and is NOT evidence for the port signal.

"THIRD-PARTY" means, exactly: consumer member != provider member. A self-tie —
a consumer whose port-resolved provider is itself — is NOT counted toward this
floor. This word is in the floor because of S-384: its headline of 12 net-new
edges was 11 self-ties and ONE genuine third-party edge, and the headline did not
say so at the point it was printed. CR-124's premise rests on that correction, so
the correction is written into the floor rather than left to the reporting.

Measured net-new third-party < 16  =>  FALSIFIED.
Measured net-new third-party >= 16 =>  the gate holds and a DELIVERY story may be
                                       planned, which is what would then create
                                       the requirement (CR-124 section 5.1).


WHY 16 — AND WHY IT IS NOT DERIVED FROM THIS CR'S OWN CENSUS
------------------------------------------------------------

1. It is S-384's floor, unchanged, on S-384's metric. CR-124 section 3.2 states
   the reason in one sentence: "matching S-384's floor and its metric so the two
   experiments are directly comparable". This gate exists to be new evidence
   against FR-WS-22's withdrawal banner, and evidence is only comparable against
   the experiment it is offered to overturn if it is measured the same way.

2. It is deliberately NOT derived from CR-124's own hand census of "39 call sites
   / 9 service pairs". That census is an upper bound produced by an unreviewed
   ad-hoc script, and CR-124 section 2 says so in its own words: "This census is
   not a finding ... it is NOT a prediction of what the product will admit, and it
   must not become an acceptance floor. That distinction is the defect S-397
   carried, where a harness census of 79 became an unreachable story criterion."
   A floor derived from the census would be a measurement of what a permissive
   reader can derive, dressed as a prediction about the measurement.

3. 16 remains above the single-consumer-coincidence threshold that justified it
   for S-384. The census attributes most of its sites to two aggregators
   (`mailbox-aggregator-api` and `funnel-aggregator-api`). A result that is one
   aggregator's configuration file and nothing else is an estate coincidence, not
   an estate-wide signal, and is not a basis for building an identity tier into
   FR-WS-20's ladder.

4. The floor is inherited, so it is NOT tuned to this signal's expected yield.
   That is a deliberate cost: a floor chosen after seeing which side of it the
   answer falls on is not a floor. If the measurement shows 16 was the wrong
   number for this signal, that is stated in the implementation notes with its
   reasoning, and THIS FILE STANDS UNCHANGED.


WHAT IS *NOT* A FLOOR
---------------------

Everything else CR-124 section 6 and S-400's acceptance criteria require is a
REPORT. None of them can clear or fail this gate on its own:

  - how many resolved base-url values carry a port, and how many do not;
  - how many ports resolve to exactly one member, to two or more, and to none,
    with the unmatched ports ENUMERATED for human adjudication;
  - the already-bound-by-path column;
  - the self-tie count, printed beside the third-party count at the headline;
  - the distinct member-to-member coupling count;
  - the literal call-site occurrence count, which is larger than the edge count
    and is reported so the census's "39 call sites" figure can be read against
    something;
  - the reconciliation against S-384's one third-party edge.

They are required for the finding to be ADJUDICABLE — they attribute a
falsification to whichever join broke — but there is exactly one decisive number,
so the gate cannot be cleared by substituting a more favourable statistic.


A RUN THAT SEES NO ESTATE REPORTS *VOID*, NEVER ZERO
-----------------------------------------------------

Per NFR-CC-04: where `LOGOS_REF_WORKSPACE` is unset the measurement has not been
performed, and 0 net-new third-party edges is not the finding — there is no
finding. The estate arm reports VOID and asserts nothing about the gate. The
classifier that decides the gate carries its own always-run coverage, so its rules
are pinned even where the estate is unavailable.


COMMITMENT
----------

This floor is not revised after the run. The constant in the measurement code is
PARSED from this file rather than compared with a literal written beside it, so
the constant cannot be edited to clear a run without this declaration being edited
too — in the file whose whole purpose is to say it must not be.

APPENDED 2026-09-14 — POST-RUN CORRECTION TO THE WORDING OF "THIRD-PARTY"
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

Appended by HUMAN DECISION at the Sprint 69 review, after the run. Everything
above this line is the declaration exactly as committed before the measurement,
and is deliberately UNCHANGED — including the COMMITMENT section, which forbids
revising it. This section neither moves the floor nor alters the verdict. It
exists because this file is the artefact a future gate will copy as a template,
and a correction recorded only in a sprint's implementation notes gets missed.

WHAT IS WRONG. The definition above reads: "THIRD-PARTY means, exactly: consumer
member != provider member", while citing S-384's split of 11 self-ties and one
genuine edge as its whole motivation. Those two are inconsistent. S-384's
self-ties are NOT consumers bound to themselves. Its finding says: "an aggregator
re-registers the callee's template on its own controller, so two members serve
it, path-only refuses, and identity binds ... identity's genuine THIRD-PARTY
contribution, where the tie is between two other members, is ONE edge." The
discriminator S-384 used is the SERVING SET -- did the consumer itself create the
ambiguity by claiming the callee's template -- not member identity. Read
literally, the wording above would score S-384's own headline as 12 genuine
edges and 0 self-ties, contradicting the sentence that justifies the word.

WHAT IT DID NOT AFFECT. Both readings were measured and printed side by side
(`Reading::AsDeclared` and `Reading::AsMeasuredByS384`). As declared: 3. As S-384
defines it: 0. The floor is 16. The finding is FALSIFIED under both readings, by
a wide margin, and no dependent story is planned under either.

WHAT A FUTURE GATE MUST DO. Any later gate declaring this metric writes S-384's
definition, not the one above: an edge counts as third-party only where the
consumer is not itself among the members serving the contested template. A gate
that inherits the wording above will report self-inflicted ambiguity as genuine
cross-service discovery, which is the exact misreading S-384's correction exists
to prevent.
