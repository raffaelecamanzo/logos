WHY DISAGREEMENT IS RARE HERE, STATED SO IT IS NOT OVER-READ. The corpus varies
its HOSTS per profile and not its PATHS: `base-url` is redefined in nearly every
test profile, `uri-*` almost never. That is why the path arm shows 2
disagreements and the base-URL arm shows 6 out of a much smaller population. A
corpus that templated its route paths per environment would measure very
differently, and this figure should not be extrapolated to one.
