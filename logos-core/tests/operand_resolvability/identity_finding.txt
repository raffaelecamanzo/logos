  12 against a floor of 16, and against CR-121's own 31.  FALSIFIED.

  AND THE 12 IS WEAKER THAN IT LOOKS. 11 of the 12 are ties the CONSUMER
  creates against ITSELF: an aggregator re-registers the callee's template on
  its own controller, so two members serve it, path-only refuses, and identity
  binds. Production resolves these the same way and in the same order
  (federation::bridge applies exactly-one over the bucket BEFORE testing whether
  the sole survivor is the consumer's own member), so the harness is faithful --
  but identity's genuine THIRD-PARTY contribution, where the tie is between two
  other members, is ONE edge.

Four sensitivities were measured rather than argued, and none rescues it:

  - SAME-TIER COLLISIONS. FR-WS-20 AC2 resolves a colliding label to nothing.
    One fork repository, archive-api-logiclens-fork, commits the original's
    Helm chart name, so the label `archive-api` resolves to neither member and
    two consumers lose every edge to it. Under a fall-through reading that
    skipped the colliding tier, net-new would be 14 — still below the floor.
    The falsification therefore does not rest on how AC2 is read.
  - METHOD-AGNOSTIC MATCHING. The consumer's HTTP verb is not recoverable from
    the composed-operand corpus, so path-only matching is compared on template
    alone. Adding the verb can only SHRINK the candidate set, which can only
    move pairs out of net-new. 12 is an upper bound, not a midpoint.
  - TEST-TREE PROVIDERS. Computed by this run, from the same walk: admitting
    src/test controllers moves routes from 94 to 105 and leaves the split
    EXACTLY unchanged at 12 net-new / 10 already-bound. Unlike CR-117's gate,
    this verdict is not an artefact of the main/test split.
  - PROVIDER COVERAGE. 25 of the 119 route-producing mapping annotations in
    ledger-gated production files yielded no readable route (21%). All 26
    unresolved pair sites sit at a template NO member serves, so supplying the
    missing provider makes them serving=1 -- already bound by path alone, never
    net-new.

WHY THE EDGES ARE NOT THERE, STATED SO IT IS NOT MIS-READ. The mechanism is
sound and the estate really does commit the evidence. Of the 32 judged pairs,
22 become edges -- 12 net-new and 10 already reachable by path alone -- and the
other 10 point at a provider whose route is invisible. That gap has one named
cause, the mirror image of the consumer-side problem CR-113 and CR-115 are
about:

  pecserver-facade registers
    value = "/mailboxes/{" + EMAIL_ADDRESS_PARAMETER_NAME + "}/size"

  The framework query captures a string-LITERAL path. A path built with `+` is
  not refused -- it is never captured, so the route is invisible and the member
  appears to serve nothing. CR-121 resolves composition on the CONSUMER side
  and inherits an unresolved composition on the PROVIDER side.

  WHAT THIS DOES NOT SAY

  It does not say the mechanism is wrong. The deploy-manifest -> via-key ->
  call-site join resolved 26 of 118 references to a call site and produced 22
  identity-resolved edges over a workspace that has 0 today. It says the
  NET-NEW yield -- the only figure that justifies eight stories whose product is
  these edges -- is 12, that 11 of those 12 are self-ties, and that the largest
  single reason for the shortfall is a provider-side composition gap CR-121 does
  not address.

  A follow-on CR that resolves composed route paths on the provider side would
  change this measurement. Re-run before re-deciding: nothing here needs to be
  re-derived by hand.
