Three sensitivities were measured rather than argued, and none rescues it:

  - SAME-TIER COLLISIONS. FR-WS-20 AC2 resolves a colliding label to nothing.
    One fork repository, archive-api-logiclens-fork, commits the original's
    Helm chart name, so the label `archive-api` resolves to neither member and
    two consumers lose every edge to it. Under a fall-through reading that
    skipped the colliding tier, net-new would be 14 — still below the floor.
    The falsification therefore does not rest on how AC2 is read.
  - METHOD-AGNOSTIC MATCHING. The consumer's HTTP verb is not recoverable from
    the composed-operand corpus, so path-only matching is compared on template
    alone. Adding the verb can only SHRINK the candidate set, which can only
    move pairs out of net-new. 12 is an upper bound, not a midpoint.
  - PROVIDER COVERAGE. 50 of the 144 mapping annotations in ledger-gated
    production files yielded no readable route (35%). All 26 unresolved pair
    sites sit at a template NO member serves, so supplying the missing provider
    makes them serving=1 -- already bound by path alone, never net-new.
  - TEST-TREE PROVIDERS. Measured, not assumed: admitting src/test controllers
    to the provider side raises routes from 94 to 105 and leaves the split
    EXACTLY unchanged at 12 net-new and 10 already-bound. Unlike CR-117's gate,
    this verdict is not an artefact of the main/test split.

WHY THE EDGES ARE NOT THERE, STATED SO IT IS NOT MIS-READ. The mechanism is
sound and the estate really does commit the evidence. Of the 32 judged pairs,
22 become edges -- 12 net-new and 10 already reachable by path alone -- and the
other 10 point at a provider whose route is invisible. That gap has one named
cause, and it is the mirror image of the consumer-side problem CR-113 and
CR-115 are about:

  pecserver-facade registers
    value = "/mailboxes/{" + EMAIL_ADDRESS_PARAMETER_NAME + "}/size"

  The framework query captures a string-LITERAL path. A path built with `+` is
  not refused -- it is never captured, so the route is invisible and the member
  appears to serve nothing. CR-121 resolves composition on the CONSUMER side
  and inherits an unresolved composition on the PROVIDER side.
