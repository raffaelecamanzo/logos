S-392 T1 — MATERIALITY FLOOR, DECLARED BEFORE THE RUN
=====================================================

Declared (UTC):  2026-09-12T12:33:41Z
Declared by:     Dev Session 3.2 (sprint-67, branch sprint-67-I3-S2)
Story:           S-392 — Measure the one-hop parameter-forwarding residue
Gate decides:    CR-121 CRA-05. Below the floor the finding is recorded as FALSIFIED
                 with its evidence and date, and S-393 and S-394 stay UNPLANNED, and
                 FR-WS-23 is marked WITHDRAWN IN PLACE (never deleted) per its own
                 GATED banner.

Estate under measurement: ~/source/pec-services (LOGOS_REF_WORKSPACE), 84 members,
read-only against the estate SOURCE. Nothing under that path is written, indexed,
re-enrolled or wiped.


THE FLOOR
---------

    >= 7 of the 13 PRODUCTION (src/main) broker publish sites resolve their topic
       operand by ONE HOP, WITHIN THE OPERAND'S OWN BUILD MODULE.

"Resolve by one hop" means, exactly and conjunctively:

  (a) the site's topic operand is a BARE PARAMETER of the enclosing method — not a
      literal (a literal needs no hop and is outside this measurement), not a field,
      not a composed expression;
  (b) that method has at least one call site recorded in the Calls ledger;
  (c) EVERY such call site lies in the SAME BUILD MODULE as the method;
  (d) EVERY such call site supplies an operand in the matching positional slot, and
      all of those operands AGREE (FR-WS-23 AC1/AC2 — disagreement emits per-site
      candidates and NO edge, so a disagreeing site does NOT count toward this floor);
  (e) the agreed operand is itself terminal at that one hop — a literal, or a
      `${…}`/`@ConfigurationProperties` accessor that FR-WS-19 resolves against
      committed configuration. An operand that is ITSELF a bare parameter of the
      caller needs a SECOND hop and does NOT count.

Measured one-hop-resolved < 7  =>  FALSIFIED.
Measured one-hop-resolved >= 7 =>  the gate holds and S-393/S-394 may be planned.

The floor of 7 fixes the maximum tolerable TWO-OR-MORE-HOP-AND-OTHER RESIDUE at
6 of 13, i.e. 46%. That is the "declared share" CR-121 §7's slope risk row requires:
above it, the broker milestone cuts to the listener side only and refuses producers
with a named reason.


WHY 7 — DERIVED FROM CR-121'S OWN FIGURES
-----------------------------------------

1. CR-121 §6 states the product under test verbatim: "On the reference workspace,
   all 16 broker listener topics resolve to literals, **the 13 production publish
   sites resolve, and `Producer` nodes rise from 0 to at least 13**". The claim
   being tested is therefore 13 of 13 — a claim about an ALREADY-ENUMERATED
   population, not an estimate over an open space.

   That 13 is not this story's invention. S-365 measured it and it is on the record
   in FR-WS-10's withdrawal note: "**0 of 13 `src/main` publish sites** would be
   admitted by canonical identity, because every one passes its topic as a method
   parameter". 54 publish sites were found; 41 are test-tree; the 38 that admitted
   are all IT test classes. The production population is 13 and is the ONLY
   population this floor is stated over.

2. The floor is one half of that claim: 13 / 2 = 6.5, rounded up to 7. This is the
   same rule S-384's identity floor applied to CR-121's other headline figure
   (31 / 2 = 15.5 -> 16), declared 2026-09-12T07:33:21Z in this same sprint. Below
   half, the hand computation behind CRA-05 is wrong by more than a factor of two —
   the failure mode CR-121 §7 names as "exactly how CR-113 died".

3. 7 is ALSO a strict majority of 13, and the two derivations converging is the
   reason 7 is the right number rather than 6 or 8. FR-WS-23 fixes the bound at
   "one level with no recursion and no fixpoint" IN ITS ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA, on the
   stated ground that `logos-core` deliberately ships no dataflow. If fewer than
   half the production sites resolve at one hop, then one hop is the EXCEPTION on
   this estate, and FR-WS-23 would be writing a minority idiom into a Must while the
   majority of the population needs precisely the dataflow slope that criterion
   exists to prevent. That is not a coverage shortfall to be topped up later; it is
   the requirement being aimed at the wrong shape.

4. The floor is a count of PRODUCTION SITES RESOLVED, not of wrappers, call sites,
   members or agreeing operands, because the site is what CR-121 promises to turn
   into a `Producer` node and what the two gated stories deliver. A high call-site
   agreement rate, a high wrapper-coverage rate, or a strong result on the no-key
   HTTP client arm does NOT clear this floor. There is exactly ONE decisive number,
   so the gate cannot be cleared by substituting a more favourable statistic.

5. Why the floor is NOT stated on the 13 as an all-or-nothing 13 of 13. CR-121 §6
   is a success criterion for the DELIVERED milestone, measured after S-393 and
   S-394 ship. This gate decides whether those stories are worth planning at all,
   and a 12-of-13 result would block a milestone that CR-121's own §7 mitigation
   would happily ship at candidate confidence. Half is the decision threshold; 13
   of 13 is the delivery target, and the two are deliberately different numbers.


WHAT IS *NOT* A FLOOR — THE REPORTS
-----------------------------------

These are required for the finding to be adjudicable and for a falsification to be
attributable to whichever join broke, but NONE of them can clear or fail this gate:

  * AC1's TEST-TREE arm. Production and test are reported separately and never
    averaged; Sprint 65 proved averaging them produces a figure with no referent —
    it is how CR-117 looked validated overall and fell on production source alone.
    A strong test-tree result cannot clear this floor. If the test-tree figure is
    reported at all it is reported beside the production one, never summed with it.

  * AC1's NO-KEY CLIENT-CALL arm. The 30 no-key production client-call sites
    (of 111: 79 resolve, 2 disagree, 30 no-key — CR-115 CRA-01, 2026-09-07) are
    measured under the same one-hop rule and REPORTED. They carry no floor because
    CR-121 attaches no promised figure to them: its only nearby number, "roughly 8
    sites", is the OVERLAY-UNION decision (§8), a different mechanism, and CR-121
    records that as a correctness decision and not a coverage one.

  * AC2's RESIDUE BREAKDOWN by reason (two-or-more hops / out-of-module / call sites
    disagree / no call sites recorded / operand not a parameter). It is the
    explanation of the headline, not a second headline.

  * AC3's CRA-06 answer (is the caller->callee lookup answerable from the existing
    Calls ledger, or does it need new storage). A NEGATIVE answer here does not
    falsify CRA-05 — it makes S-393 more expensive, which is a planning input, not
    a gate. It is reported as its own verdict with its own evidence.

  * AC5's MEASURED COST of the hop. A figure for the perf reconciliation. A slow
    hop is a budget conversation, not a falsification.


NON-VACUITY PRECONDITIONS — "VOID" IS NOT "FALSIFIED"
-----------------------------------------------------

A zero produced by a walk that walked nothing reads identically to a zero produced
by a walk that walked everything. The run is VOID — neither confirming nor
falsifying, and the gate must be re-run — unless ALL of these hold, each asserted
NON-ZERO by the harness:

  V1. The walk admits a non-zero number of estate members (expected 84).
  V2. The walk admits a non-zero number of production Java source files, and the
      broker detector gates a non-zero number of them.
  V3. The walk finds AT LEAST 13 production (src/main) publish sites. Finding
      FEWER than the enumerated population means the harness is not looking at what
      S-365 counted, and the result is VOID, not FALSIFIED.
  V4. The Calls ledger yields a non-zero number of call rows over the production
      tree as a whole.

V4 is deliberately scoped to the tree, not to the wrapper methods. Zero call rows
ACROSS THE WHOLE PRODUCTION TREE is a broken walk => VOID. Zero call rows FOR THE
WRAPPER METHODS SPECIFICALLY, while the rest of the tree has them, is a real
measured finding about CRA-06 and counts as residue under AC2 — it is reported,
not voided.


A CAVEAT DECLARED IN ADVANCE, NOT A FLOOR
-----------------------------------------

If the resolved sites all funnel through a SINGLE wrapper method, or sit in a
SINGLE member, then the 13-of-13 claim rests on one syntactic data point repeated,
and the generality of the inference is correspondingly weak. The harness therefore
reports the number of DISTINCT wrapper methods and DISTINCT members behind the
resolved count. This is stated here, before the run, so that it is read as a
declared limit on what a PASS licenses — not as a second threshold invented
afterwards to argue away an inconvenient result. It does not clear or fail the
gate.


COMMITMENT
----------

This floor is not revised after the run. If the measurement shows the floor was
badly chosen, that is stated in the implementation notes with the reasoning, and
THIS FILE STANDS UNCHANGED.
